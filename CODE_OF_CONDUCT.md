# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a welcoming place for students, educators, makers, and robotics developers. Be respectful, constructive, patient with different experience levels, and precise when discussing safety or technical evidence.

Examples of positive participation include:

- explaining the reasoning and evidence behind a suggestion;
- separating confirmed facts from assumptions and pending hardware tests;
- giving actionable, kind review feedback;
- protecting personal information and physical safety;
- crediting prior work and respecting third-party licenses.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation, unsafe pressure to operate hardware, plagiarism, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile or another private channel they publish. Do not disclose another person's sensitive information in a public Issue.

Maintainers will consider context, impact, and repeated behavior, and will handle reports as consistently and confidentially as practical.
